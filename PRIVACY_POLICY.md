# Privacy Policy — Sector Zero: Lockdown

Effective date: September 25, 2026  
Package: `com.targetzero.lockdown`

This policy describes data handling in the current Android version of Sector Zero: Lockdown. The game uses Google Mobile Ads to display banner and optional rewarded advertisements. It does not use a developer-operated account or gameplay server.

## Game data stored on your device

Game progress and settings, such as mission progress, weapon upgrades, earned in-game currency, and preferences, are saved in the app's private storage on your device. The game code does not send this save data to a developer server. You can remove local saves by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game.

## Google AdMob and consent choices

If you choose “Agree” in the in-app advertising disclosure, the game can use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) for ads. Google says that this SDK automatically collects and shares:

- Approximate location: your IP address, which may be used to estimate general location. The game does not request precise GPS location for ads.
- App interactions: interactions such as app launches, taps, and ad/video views.
- Diagnostics: technical and performance information, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage.
- Device or account identifiers: Android advertising ID, app set ID, and, where applicable, identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device.

Google uses these data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google may process the data under its own privacy terms; see the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google advertising privacy information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

The game saves your advertising choice locally so it does not ask again on every launch. On first use, before any ad or UMP consent request, it shows its own disclosure with “Agree and continue” and “Not now — continue without ads.” Choosing “Not now” leaves gameplay available and prevents the game from requesting ads. You can change the choice from Settings → Privacy & Ad Choices. If you agree, the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK presents privacy messages and handles consent choices where required. If Google requires a privacy-options form for your region, a separate Google Privacy Options button is also available in Settings.

## Other information

The game does not ask you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. In this version, game progress is local only; there is no developer-operated analytics, crash-reporting, sign-in, or purchase service.

## Choices, access, and deletion

You can decline ads or change your choice in the game's Settings. You can also reset or delete your Android advertising ID in Android settings; that control does not necessarily erase other data Google may already have processed. The developer does not receive or store the AdMob data described above on a developer server and therefore cannot directly retrieve or delete Google's copies. For information or requests concerning data Google processes, use Google's privacy controls and policies linked above.

## Children

Sector Zero: Lockdown is an action shooter marketed to older audiences and is not designed for children under 13. Its Google Play age rating and target-audience information are shown on its store listing. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to the developer, contact us.
